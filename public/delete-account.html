Data & Account Management

Control your personal information and content

At Hidden Pantry, we believe you should have full control over your data. You can choose to delete specific content or your entire account and associated data.

🗑️ Deleting Specific Data

You can remove specific types of information without deleting your entire account:

1. Deleting Recipes (Posts)

  • Go to the My Recipes section in your profile.
  • Select the recipe you wish to remove.
  • Tap the Options (three dots) and select Delete Recipe.
  • The recipe, its images, and all related nutritional data will be permanently removed.

2. Clearing Chat Messages

  • Open the chat conversation you wish to clear.
  • Tap the Menu icon in the top right corner.
  • Select Clear Chat to remove all messages from your view and our active records.

3. Removing Saved Content

  • Go to your Bookmarks or Cookbooks.
  • Swipe left or tap the 'Unsave' icon on any recipe to remove it from your collection.

⚠️ Permanent Account Deletion

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. All your data will be purged immediately.

To delete your entire account and all associated data (Profile, Recipes, History, and Settings):

  • Open the app and go to Profile Settings.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm the prompt. Your data will be deleted on the first attempt with no recovery option.

📊 Data Policy

What we delete:

  • Identity: Name, email, profile picture, and hashed password.
  • Content: All recipes, posts, comments, and reviews you have created.
  • Interaction: Chat messages, saved bookmarks, and nutrition plans.

What we may keep:

  • Legal: Transaction records for billing (Google/Apple) are kept as required by tax laws.
  • Security: Anonymized system logs for security and performance audits.

Retention Period: In-app deletions take effect immediately. Manual email requests are processed within 7 business days.
