About Hidden Pantry

Hidden Pantry is a comprehensive nutrition and recipe platform that connects users with certified nutritionists for personalised dietary guidance.

Our Mission

To make professional nutrition advice accessible to everyone, while providing a platform for nutritionists to grow their practice and reach more clients.

Key Features

  • Smart Recipe Discovery — Browse thousands of recipes with intelligent search powered by pantry-based ingredient matching and TF-IDF recommendations.
  • Nutritionist Marketplace — Find and subscribe to certified nutritionists across various specialisations including Clinical Nutrition, Sports Nutrition, Weight Management, and more.
  • Personalised Meal Plans — Receive custom meal plans tailored to your dietary needs, goals, and preferences directly from your nutritionist.
  • Real-Time Consultations — Chat with your nutritionist, schedule meetings, and receive ongoing support through our integrated messaging system.
  • Cooking Assistant — Step-by-step cooking guidance with text-to-speech instructions and dynamic serving size adjustments.
  • Offline Access — Download your favourite recipes for offline use, perfect for cooking without internet access.

Technology

Built with Flutter and powered by Firebase, Hidden Pantry leverages modern cloud infrastructure for real-time data synchronisation, secure payments via Stripe Connect, and intelligent recipe recommendations through our custom TF-IDF backend.
